### *Building practical AI systems that deliver measurable business value.*

Welcome to **Sergio Innovation Labs**, a collection of **production‑ready AI systems** designed to solve real business problems in **Finance, Crypto, Trading, and Fintech**.

## 🔧 Core Projects

### Crypto Technical Analysis RAG Assistant
A domain‑specialized assistant trained on Supply & Demand, Dow Theory, Elliott Wave, and technical indicators.  
Transforms personal TA notes and PDFs into a searchable, intelligent knowledge system.

### Whitepaper & Macro News Analyzer
An advanced RAG pipeline that extracts insights from crypto whitepapers and contextualizes them with macroeconomic signals.

### Market Intelligence Dashboard
A real‑time crypto market intelligence platform combining:
- live market data  
- technical analysis  
- sentiment extraction  
- LLM‑driven reasoning  

### Fintech Compliance Document Helper
An intelligent document‑processing system for KYC, AML, and regulatory workflows — built using real‑world banking and compliance experience.

### Trading Research Agent (Capstone)
A full‑stack, multi‑agent research system integrating:
- live market data  
- TA logic  
- macroeconomic intelligence  
- hybrid LLMs (cloud + local)  
- workflow orchestration  

This is the flagship project demonstrating end‑to‑end AI solution architecture.
